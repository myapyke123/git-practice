# Link about Software Development 
https://thevaluable.dev/programmer-stress-causes/

This article is written by a man who was at risk of losing his job as a software developer. He talks about being restless and feeling like his situration was life or death. He then runs through the different streeses that he faces as a software developer. I really enjoyed this article because it taps into a side of this career that isn't normally transparent. Developing for me IS stressful, so to see someone else agree in such great detail was an amazing read. 

## Comment from James Huang

I agree that stress is an important but often overlooked part of software development. What I found especially interesting is that the stress does not necessarily come only from writing difficult code, but also from things like deadlines, uncertainty, and the pressure to constantly learn new technologies. As computer science students, we often focus on improving our technical skills, so it is interesting to think about how managing stress and maintaining a healthy work-life balance can also be important skills for a long-term career in software development.

— **James Huang**
